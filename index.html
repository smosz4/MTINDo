Our Services

MTINDO provides comprehensive educational solutions designed to support learners, teachers and schools in achieving academic excellence.

Seminars

Educational Seminars

Subject-based seminars for learners focusing on examination preparation, concept mastery, competence development and problem-solving skills.

Training

Teacher Training

Professional development programmes for teachers in CBC implementation, assessment, teaching methodologies, classroom management and more.

Consultancy

School Consultancy

Comprehensive consultancy services to schools including curriculum implementation, assessment systems, academic performance improvement and digital learning.

Assessments

Examinations & Assessment

Development of mock examinations, end-of-term exams, continuous assessment items and revision materials that measure knowledge and application.

Competitions

Academic Competitions

Our Philosophy

Know. Understand. Learn. Seek. Find Answers. Create Wealth.

MTINDO's vision is inspired by six action-oriented concepts that guide how we work with learners, teachers and schools.

MANYA - TEGERA - IYIGE - NOONYA - DDAMU - OBUGAGA

Know - Knowledge is the foundation of learning.
Understand - Move beyond memorisation to genuine understanding.
Learn - Learning is a continuous process of acquiring and applying knowledge.
Seek - Encourage curiosity, research and pursuit of information.
Find Answers - Guide learners to investigate problems and develop solutions.
Create Wealth - Knowledge and education are forms of wealth that transform lives.

Founder & Education Consultant

Okot Martine is an experienced education professional and consultant with deep expertise in English Language, Literature in English, and General Paper. He has over 8 years of teaching experience and currently heads the English Department at Caring Heart Secondary School in Wakiso.

As an education consultant, Okot brings practical classroom insights into MTINDO's programmes, ensuring all solutions are grounded in real teaching and learning experiences. He is passionate about bridging the gaps that exist in education and empowering learners, teachers and schools to achieve excellence.
