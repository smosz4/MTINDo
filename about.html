About MTINDO

MTINDO Educational Solutions is an education consultancy and solutions provider established to respond to some of the challenges faced by learners, teachers and schools. We believe every learner can excel when provided with the right knowledge, guidance, teaching, materials and opportunities.

The MTINDO Acronym

What MTINDO Means

The name MTINDO is inspired by a series of action-oriented concepts that guide how we support learners, teachers and schools in their educational journey.

M
MANYA
Know

Knowledge is the foundation of learning. We help build strong knowledge bases across all subjects and competencies.

T
TEGERA
Understand

We encourage learners to move beyond memorisation to genuine understanding of concepts and their applications.

I
IYIGE
Learn

Learning is a continuous process of acquiring and applying knowledge in practical and meaningful ways.

N
NOONYA
Seek

We encourage curiosity, research, investigation and the active pursuit of information and solutions.

D

Our programmes are designed to complement classroom learning and provide practical solutions to educational challenges. Whether through seminars, training, consultancy or digital learning, MTINDO brings expertise, innovation and a commitment to making education better for everyone.
