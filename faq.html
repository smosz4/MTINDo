Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MTINDO, our programmes, services and how to register.

About MTINDO
What is MTINDO Educational Solutions?
+

MTINDO is an education-focused organisation that provides innovative, practical and reliable solutions to learners, teachers, schools and education stakeholders. We specialize in academic seminars, teacher training, examinations, educational consultancy, competitions, digital learning and scholastic materials. Our mission is to bridge gaps in teaching and learning and enhance academic excellence.

What does "WE ARE THE REMEDY" mean?
+

"We Are The Remedy" is MTINDO's motto, reflecting our commitment to providing solutions to the missing links in the corridor of excellence. We believe that every educational challenge has a practical solution, and MTINDO is here to provide those solutions to learners, teachers and schools.

How long has MTINDO been operating?
+

MTINDO was established to respond to educational challenges faced by learners, teachers and schools. We have worked with multiple schools across Uganda and continue to expand our reach and services to serve more educational stakeholders.

Our Services
What services does MTINDO offer?
+

MTINDO offers a wide range of educational services including: Educational Seminars, Teacher Training & Professional Development, School Consultancy, Examinations & Assessment, General Paper & Research Programmes, English Language & Literature support, Online Learning, Scholastic Materials, Academic Competitions, Debate & Public Speaking, Co-curricular programmes, Practical Learning, and Career Guidance. Visit our Services page for detailed information about each offering.

Can MTINDO customize programmes for my school?
+

Yes! MTINDO specializes in providing customized educational solutions for schools. Schools can contact us to discuss their specific needs, challenges and academic goals. We will design programmes tailored to your school's curriculum and student requirements. Contact us via WhatsApp or phone to discuss your school's needs.

Does MTINDO provide online learning programmes?
+

Yes! MTINDO Online School provides opportunities for learners to access lessons, revision classes, holiday learning programmes, and examination preparation beyond their physical classrooms. Online programmes can be tailored to different subjects and academic levels depending on what suits your needs.

Programmes & Registration
Who can participate in MTINDO programmes?
+

MTINDO programmes are designed for a wide range of participants including Primary learners, O-Level and A-Level students, Primary and Secondary teachers, School administrators, and Parents seeking educational support for their children. Each programme specifies its target group. If you're unsure whether a programme is suitable for you, contact us.

+

Each MTINDO programme has its own registration deadline, usually 3-7 days before the event. Specific deadlines are listed on each programme's details page. Early registration is recommended as many programmes have limited spots. Contact us for late registration inquiries.

+

Absolutely! Schools can invite MTINDO to organize customized seminars, training workshops, competitions or other educational programmes suited to their academic calendar and specific needs. Contact us to discuss the details, dates, subjects and arrangements. We'll work with you to deliver a high-quality programme for your learners and teachers.

Teachers & Professional Development
Does MTINDO offer training for teachers?
+

Yes! MTINDO provides comprehensive professional development programmes for teachers. Training areas include CBC Implementation, Assessment and assessment item writing, Continuous assessment, Teaching methodologies, Classroom management, English Language teaching, Literature teaching, Research methodology, Educational technology, and Scheme of work preparation. Teachers can join public workshops or schools can arrange in-house training.

Can teachers join MTINDO programmes individually?
+

Yes! Teachers can participate as individuals in MTINDO seminars and training programmes. Additionally, schools can arrange group participation for their teaching staff. Contact us to inquire about upcoming teacher training events or to arrange a customized workshop for your school.

Is there a refund policy?
