Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

MTINDO Educational Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website (mtindoeducation.com), register for our programmes, contact us, or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

We may collect the following information when you provide it to us:

  • Registration Information: Full name, phone number, email address, school/organisation, class/position
  • Contact Form Information: Name, email, phone, message, service inquiries
  • Programme Registration: Registration details, payment information, special requirements
  • Communication: Any messages you send us via email, WhatsApp, phone or contact forms
  • Photos/Videos: With appropriate permission for testimonials, gallery or case studies

Information Collected Automatically

Our website may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and managing programme registrations
  • Sending programme updates, schedules and reminders
  • Responding to inquiries and customer support
  • Sending promotional information about new programmes (with consent)
  • Improving our website and services
  • Analyzing website usage and performance
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Creating testimonials and case studies (with permission)
  • Managing payments and transactions

3. Data Protection

MTINDO takes data security seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Schools and educational partners when coordinating programmes (with your consent)
  • Payment processors for transaction processing
  • Service providers who assist us in operating our website and services
  • Legal authorities if required by law

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request information about how your data is used

To exercise these rights, please contact us at mtindoedusol@gmail.com or call 0785 492591.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us recognize you and improve our services. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Some website features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

7. Children's Privacy

Our programmes and services may involve learners under 18 years old. When collecting information from minors, we:

  • Collect only necessary information
  • Seek parental/guardian consent where appropriate
  • Do not share identifiable information without permission
  • Protect children's privacy and safety

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. MTINDO is not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites before providing your information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our website and services following changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
